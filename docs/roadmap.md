# Delivery roadmap

## Milestone 1 — implemented

Static platform, local persistence, concept prerequisites, extensible content types, numeric and choice grading, rubric self-grading, portable progress, reviewed LLM exchange, and Statistics Week 1.

## Milestone 2 — Statistics course

Author and mathematically review Weeks 2–8 with 15–20 core problems per week, challenge derivations, quizzes, worked solutions, and remediation. Add the Week 4 midterm (about 50 points / 60 minutes) and Week 8 cumulative final (about 80 points / 90 minutes). Extend the content engine for explicit multi-part question groups and assessment sessions, weighting and session summaries. Add sampling/CLT interactive explorations. Expand spaced retrieval selection based on missed concept evidence.

## Foundational algebra — introductory modules implemented

Pre-algebra and Algebra I: First Principles now have eight teaching guides, 80 questions, diagnostics, checkpoints, worked solutions, and written-response rubrics. The platform now uses a shared course registry, course-specific navigation/progress, and cross-course learning evidence. Next algebra modules: systems of equations, polynomials and factoring, and quadratics.

## Milestone 3 — curriculum expansion

Further algebra, proof foundations, calculus, and linear algebra with shared stable concepts. Expand the course and concept dependency graph beyond the initial statistics slice. Keep planned content visibly distinct from available material. Advanced proofs retain rubric-based evaluation; symbolic equivalence and automated proof checking are not implied.

## Platform follow-up

Browser interaction/accessibility QA, multi-tab conflict handling, migration fixtures, optional offline service worker, and optional user-controlled synchronization. Authorship and curriculum review should be explicit before claiming a complete advanced-mathematics curriculum.
